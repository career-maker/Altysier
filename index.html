Insights & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Altysier Group operates across international trade, industrial investment, manufacturing, transportation, agriculture, food security, healthcare and mobility, structured as a diversified portfolio of independent companies.

The Group is made up of five companies: Altysier International General Trading, Altysier International for Advanced Business, Al Mutmeiza for Industries Investment, Altysier for Transport and Services, and Haloub Feed Mill Factory.

Our companies operate across a growing number of regional and international markets, connecting supply chains and distribution networks across multiple countries.

We work with suppliers, distributors, investors and industrial partners across trade, manufacturing, logistics and healthcare, building long-term commercial relationships grounded in trust and reliable execution.
