OVERVIEW

Company Overview

About Al Taysir Al Mutamayyiza Gulf Co.

A precision overland transport and freight provider operating critical supply pipelines across Saudi Arabia and cross-border GCC corridors.

Al Taysir Al Mutamayyiza Gulf Co. provides scalable, technology-driven transportation and freight forwarding solutions. From heavy industrial transport to fast airport ground logistics, we ensure seamless flow of goods between seaports, air cargo hubs, and industrial distribution terminals.

Our fleet features advanced GPS telematics, temperature-controlled trailers, and certified logistics professionals who handle customs clearance, containerized transport, and contracted project cargo under uncompromising safety protocols.

PURPOSE

Our Journey

Built With Purpose

Expanding transport corridors with reliable fleet execution and Gulf-wide connectivity.

Phase 01 / Fleet Inception

Origin

Established to service rapid overland transport demands across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, connecting major dry ports and shipping hubs with dedicated cargo fleets.

Phase 02 / Cross-Border Corridors

Development

Introduced cross-border GCC trucking permits, refrigerated cold-chain trailers, airport air-freight ground handling contracts, and centralized bonded warehousing facilities.

Phase 03 / Strategic Logistics

Current Role

Acts as the physical logistics backbone for Altysier Group and independent corporate clients, delivering timely transport solutions for commercial cargo, FMCG, and heavy machinery.

TRANSPORT

Core Business

Key Logistics Areas

Modern transport, warehousing, and freight management engineered for reliability.

01

Overland Road Transportation

Long-haul intercity freight and cross-border GCC trucking using flatbeds, curtain-siders, bulk grain trailers, and temperature-controlled reefer trucks.

02

Airport Ground Freight & Services

Air-side and land-side freight transfers, unit load device (ULD) transport, expedited customs transit, and time-critical medical or perishable air cargo shuttles.

03

Customs Brokerage & Clearance

Expert regulatory documentation, duty assessment, and expedited port-of-entry clearance across King Abdulaziz Port, Jeddah Islamic Port, and King Fahd Causeway.

04

Warehousing & Cargo Staging

Secure, 24/7 monitored dry and climate-controlled storage hubs enabling cross-docking, consolidation, barcode pallet tracking, and flexible distribution buffering.

CAPABILITY

Core Advantage

Cross-Border Efficiency & Real-Time Fleet Telematics

Every vehicle in our modern fleet is outfitted with GPS tracking and cold-chain temperature telemetry, giving our commercial clients live transit visibility, route optimization, and assured delivery compliance across high-value shipments.
