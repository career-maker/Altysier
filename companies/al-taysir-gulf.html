OVERVIEW

Company Overview

About Al Taysir Al Mutamayyiza Gulf Co.

A premier logistical powerhouse operating an advanced commercial fleet, bonded warehouses, and cross-border transport corridors.

Headquartered in Saudi Arabia, Al Taysir Al Mutamayyiza Gulf Co. provides end-to-end transport and logistics services for industrial manufacturers, retail distributors, and international shipping entities. Our operations bridge critical land ports, maritime terminals, and dry cargo hubs.

Driven by precision fleet management and strict regulatory compliance, we deliver time-critical, temperature-sensitive, and oversized industrial cargo with complete visibility and operational transparency.

PURPOSE

Our Journey

Built With Purpose

From specialized regional trucking to an integrated regional logistics and freight network.

Phase 01 / Logistics Foundation

Origin

Established in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia to satisfy accelerating industrial transport demands between major seaport gateways and inland commercial manufacturing zones.

Phase 02 / Fleet & Hub Scale

Development

Expanded modern heavy-haul fleets, invested in ambient and temperature-controlled warehousing, and established dedicated customs-clearance desks at key borders.

Phase 03 / Regional Supply Spine

Current Role

Serves as a strategic logistics partner across the Gulf region, managing daily scheduled freight runs, port transshipment, and high-value project logistics.

SERVICES

Core Capabilities

Key Service Areas

Comprehensive ground, air, and marine logistics solutions customized for modern supply chains.

01

End-to-End Logistics & Transport

Full truckload (FTL), less-than-truckload (LTL), and specialized flatbed heavy-haul transport across interconnected highways throughout Saudi Arabia and the GCC.

02

Warehousing & Storage Solutions

Secured, modern warehousing facilities equipped with digital WMS inventory tracking, pallet racking, climate control, and pick-and-pack fulfillment systems.

03

Port, Customs & Free Zone Storage

Expedited customs clearance, bonded cargo storage, container handling, and seamless port-to-warehouse drayage at major coastal gateways and dry ports.

04

Airport Logistics & Ground Services

Air freight coordination, tarmac cargo dispatch, priority courier expediting, and time-critical medical and high-tech component transport from major international airports.

Core Advantage

Equipped with telematics-monitored vehicles, dedicated safety protocols, and veteran drivers, we guarantee on-time delivery across extensive desert and urban corridors, minimizing downtime and supply friction for our corporate partners.
