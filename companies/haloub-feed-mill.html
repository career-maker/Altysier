Haloub Feed Mill Factory

Pioneering animal nutrition, livestock health, and regional food security through automated industrial feed milling, sustainable raw materials, and precision science.

OVERVIEW

Company Overview

About Haloub Feed Mill Factory

A modern industrial manufacturing complex producing nutritionally balanced, high-yield feed formulations for cattle, poultry, and sheep.

Haloub Feed Mill Factory operates state-of-the-art milling, mixing, pelleting, and bagging lines. Built to bolster national agriculture, the plant processes locally harvested sorghum, bran, oilseed meal, and imported premixes into scientifically verified animal feed.

By providing commercial livestock breeders, dairy farms, and poultry producers with cost-effective, high-protein nutrition, Haloub directly supports meat production yields, milk output, and food sovereignty across the region.

PURPOSE

Our Journey

Built With Purpose

From targeted agrarian manufacturing to an industrial agricultural anchor.

Phase 01 / Factory Inception

Origin

Commissioned to substitute expensive animal feed imports by processing domestic agricultural grains into high-quality animal fodder and concentrated pellets.

Phase 02 / Automation & Capacity

Development

Upgraded to fully automated PLC-controlled batching lines, high-temperature conditioning, computerized pellet presses, and an on-site wet chemical laboratory.

Phase 03 / National Food Security

Current Role

Supplies thousands of tons of poultry, dairy, and livestock feed monthly, acting as an indispensable partner to large agribusinesses and pastoral communities.

MANUFACTURING

Core Operations

Key Business Areas

Scientific feed engineering, uncompromising safety controls, and sustainable processing.

01

Animal Feed Manufacturing

Industrial-scale production of specialized starter, grower, and finisher pellets for broilers and layers, as well as high-energy ruminant concentrates for dairy cows and fattening sheep.

02

Quality & Safety Control Systems

Rigorous on-site testing for mycotoxins, moisture levels, digestible protein percentages, and mineral balances to ensure zero contamination and peak feed-conversion ratios (FCR).

03

Livestock & Agricultural Support

On-farm nutritional advisory services, herd feeding consultation, and contract grain purchasing agreements with local farming cooperatives to ensure agricultural prosperity.

04

Sustainable Local Production

Minimizing agro-industrial waste by upcycling oilseed byproducts, sunflower cakes, and molasses into high-value animal nutrition that supports circular local economies.

CAPABILITY

Core Advantage

Sustainable Local Production & Nutritional Precision

Haloub delivers an optimal Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) through laboratory-proven formulations. We lower production costs for farmers while accelerating livestock growth cycles, safeguarding herd health, and securing food chains.
