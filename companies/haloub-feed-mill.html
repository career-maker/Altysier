OVERVIEW

Company Overview

About Haloub Feed Mill Factory

A premier agro-industrial manufacturing asset delivering scientifically formulated, bio-secure nutrition for commercial livestock and poultry operations.

Haloub Feed Mill Factory stands as a premier modern production complex dedicated to the manufacturing of pelleted, mashed, and specialty feed concentrates. Designed according to European hygienic standards, our facilities combine computerized batching, multi-stage micro-ingredient dosing, and automated steam conditioning.

We work directly with commercial poultry integrators, dairy producers, and livestock pastoralists, formulating balanced rations that optimize animal conversion ratios, health resilience, and economic yields for local and export markets.

PURPOSE

Our Journey

Built With Purpose

From local agricultural milling to an automated, high-output nutritional plant.

Phase 01 / Factory Foundation

Origin

Established to reduce dependency on imported animal feeds and address severe feed conversion inefficiencies across domestic poultry and dairy farms.

Phase 02 / Automation & Upgrades

Development

Invested in advanced automated pelleting lines, high-capacity grain storage silos, and an in-house laboratory for continuous NIR protein and moisture testing.

Phase 03 / National Food Security

Current Role

Produces high-tonnage monthly feed outputs, empowering livestock farming clusters, pastoral communities, and regional food protein supply chains.

PRODUCTION

Core Capabilities

Key Production Areas

Formulating balanced, scientific feed rations across poultry, dairy, and livestock sectors.

01

Poultry Starter, Grower & Layer Feeds

High-energy, amino-acid balanced mash and crumble rations formulated for maximum broiler weight gain, low mortality, and high egg production rates.

02

Ruminant & Dairy Concentrates

Steam-pelleted concentrates for dairy cows, fattening bulls, sheep, and camels, fortified with essential minerals, bypass proteins, and digestive enzymes.

03

Grain & Ingredient Processing

Direct procurement of yellow corn, soybean meal, wheat bran, and mineral premixes from certified agricultural suppliers with strict mycotoxin screening.

04

Custom Formulation & Veterinary Support

Bespoke feed recipes designed by veterinary nutritionists tailored to specific herd health profiles, climatic stress conditions, and target fattening cycles.

CAPABILITY

Core Advantage

Nutritional Precision & Certified Biosecurity

From computerized ingredient weighing to thermal pellet treatment, every batch from Haloub Feed Mill undergoes rigorous laboratory validation to ensure zero salmonella contamination, optimal nutritional balance, and proven feed conversion improvements.
