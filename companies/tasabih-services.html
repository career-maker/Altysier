OVERVIEW

Company Overview

About TASABIH for Service and Transportation Co. Ltd.

A versatile corporate execution vehicle managing heavy transport fleets, strategic procurement services, and structured general trading.

TASABIH for Service and Transportation Co. Ltd. operates as a dynamic operational provider, offering heavy commercial road haulage, contracted industrial fleet services, and corporate procurement for large commercial enterprises.

With an agile structure and extensive logistics network, TASABIH handles project logistics, specialized cargo movements, and tailored general trading transactions, bridging supply gaps with high operational discipline and total transparency.

PURPOSE

Our Journey

Built With Purpose

From specialized haulage services to an integrated multi-service trading provider.

Phase 01 / Fleet Genesis

Origin

Formed to provide reliable commercial cargo hauling for heavy industrial clients facing chronic equipment shortages and delivery delays.

Phase 02 / Services Integration

Development

Expanded into commercial corporate procurement, equipment leasing, and general trading, creating a single-window operational partner for regional projects.

Phase 03 / Operations Backbone

Current Role

Acts as a responsive service and transportation arm within the Altysier ecosystem, delivering specialized transit solutions and cross-industry trade support.

SERVICES

Core Capabilities

Key Business Areas

Combining transport fleets, general trading services, and corporate procurement under one roof.

01

Commercial Road Transportation

Heavy tractor-trailers, bulk flatbeds, and box trucks servicing intercity industrial cargo routes, construction project hauls, and scheduled commodity transfers.

02

Integrated Corporate Support

Turnkey fleet leasing, dedicated driver staffing, vehicle maintenance agreements, and site-level logistical support for industrial contractors and public enterprises.

03

General Trading & Supply

Sourcing and delivery of commercial goods, construction materials, industrial hardware, and consumable inventory tailored to specific enterprise tenders.

04

Project Logistics Management

Comprehensive route surveys, oversize load escorts, off-road equipment positioning, and coordinated staging for infrastructure and energy projects.

CAPABILITY

Core Advantage

Operational Versatility & End-to-End Fleet Control

We combine our own heavy vehicle fleet with nimble procurement trading capabilities. This enables TASABIH to provide full-cycle solutions — sourcing materials, managing customs and staging, and delivering directly to project sites without third-party handoffs.
