OVERVIEW

Company Overview

About TASABIH for Service and Transportation Co. Ltd.

A versatile industrial support enterprise providing high-capacity transportation, corporate supplies, and field operations management.

TASABIH for Service and Transportation Co. Ltd. functions as an agile operational backbone for industrial enterprises, infrastructure contractors, and public sector organizations. We combine an extensive fleet of heavy haulage transport vehicles with comprehensive corporate procurement capabilities.

Whether moving bulk raw materials across remote corridors, managing specialized commercial trading agreements, or handling facility support services, TASABIH provides the reliability and precision required to keep large-scale operations on track.

PURPOSE

Our Journey

Built With Purpose

From specialized logistics support to full-spectrum corporate procurement and transport.

Phase 01 / Fleet Genesis

Origin

Formed to resolve severe heavy-duty transit shortages for major industrial projects, assembling a dedicated fleet of flatbeds, tippers, and cargo haulers.

Phase 02 / Service Integration

Development

Expanded into general trading operations and corporate procurement, managing bulk material supply contracts for construction, mining, and agricultural entities.

Phase 03 / Multi-Service Engine

Current Role

Stands as a trusted multi-service contractor, handling high-stakes logistics runs, government supply frameworks, and comprehensive enterprise fleet solutions.
