Responsibility Beyond Business

Altysier Group conducts business with an enduring sense of stewardship — fostering ethical trade, empowering local employment, securing essential food systems, and operating with continuous responsibility across regional markets.

ETHICS

Pillar 01 · Corporate Governance & Trust

Doing Business the Right Way

We believe lasting enterprise value is impossible without rigorous transparency, audited compliance, and deep mutual respect across all commercial relationships.

In complex cross-border trade, integrity is not merely a policy — it is our operational lifeline. From commodity procurement to municipal infrastructure contracts, Altysier Group holds every employee, operating subsidiary, and commercial partner to clear ethical standards.

We maintain stringent anti-corruption practices, clear contract terms, fully verifiable customs declarations, and transparent pricing structures that safeguard both our partners and the communities we serve.

01 / INTEGRITY

Fair & Transparent Trading

Clear contractual terms, zero hidden intermediary commissions, and open market rate disclosures across all import-export transactions.

02 / TRACEABILITY

Responsible Sourcing

Audited supply chains, verified direct grower and factory agreements, and adherence to legal origin certifications across commodities.

03 / COMPLIANCE

Regulatory Adherence

Comprehensive customs manifest verification, international sanctions screening, and full tax and banking compliance in all jurisdictions.

04 / PARTNERSHIP

Respectful Partnerships

Equitable financial terms and timely settlements with smallholder suppliers, regional logistics contractors, and institutional buyers.

05 / LEADERSHIP

Accountable Governance

Active executive oversight, clear lines of accountability across group subsidiaries, and zero tolerance for bribery or conflicts of interest.

GROWTH

Pillar 02 · Economic Empowerment & Jobs

Creating Opportunity. Supporting Growth.

Empowering Livelihoods

We build commercial pathways that create real family incomes, foster self-employed entrepreneurs, and strengthen domestic business ecosystems.

Sustainable economic impact isn't abstract charity — it is built by giving individuals and enterprises the tools, transport, and capital access they need to thrive independently.

Supporting Small Businesses

Flexible commercial credit terms, micro-distributor arrangements, and reliable inventory supply for local shops and traders.

Mobility Solutions

Nationwide BAJAJ rickshaw distribution providing thousands of self-employed drivers with dependable daily passenger and cargo income.

Skilled Job Creation

Full-time careers in mechanical assembly, specialized fleet operation, industrial grain processing, and logistics management.

Strengthening Supply Chains

Connecting regional rural producers directly with high-volume urban markets, reducing waste and ensuring fair purchase prices.

SECURITY

Pillar 03 · Agricultural Vitality

Strengthening Food Security

Haloub Feed Mill Factory

Through Haloub Feed Mill Factory, we directly safeguard regional livestock populations, support crop farmers, and stabilize essential food supplies.

Food security in developing markets requires modern local manufacturing. By producing balanced animal feed locally, Altysier Group shields regional farmers from unpredictable global supply disruptions while enhancing rural nutritional security.

Scientific Animal Feed Production

Computerized batching and precision nutrient formulas that significantly lower mortality rates in poultry and dairy farming.

Livestock Health & Welfare

High-protein feed ratios that strengthen cattle immunity, accelerate healthy growth, and support sustainable livestock breeding.

Agricultural Off-Take Support

Guaranteed bulk crop purchasing from domestic farmers for sorghum, maize, and bran, infusing capital directly into rural farms.

Regional Food Supply Stability

Consistent local meat, egg, and dairy production, protecting families from external commodity price volatility.

OPERATIONS

Pillar 04 · Operational Footprint

Operating With Greater Responsibility

Practical, measurable operational stewardship embedded into our haulage fleets, factories, and facilities.

01 / LOGISTICS

Optimized Logistics & Transport

Route optimization software, backhaul load matching to eliminate empty trips, and regular engine maintenance that curbs fuel consumption across Al Taysir Gulf and TASABIH fleets.

02 / MANUFACTURING

Clean Plant Operations

Closed-loop cyclone filtration systems to trap fine organic dust, high-efficiency electric milling motors, and strict spill prevention protocols at our processing plants.

03 / RESOURCES

Responsible Resource Use

Reusable wooden pallets, bulk recyclable packaging, energy audits across logistics warehouses, and minimized water use in factory cleaning cycles.

04 / WORKSHOPS

Safe Waste Handling

Certified disposal of automotive lubricants, battery recycling programs, and metal scrap recovery across our BAJAJ assembly and repair workshops.
